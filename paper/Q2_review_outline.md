# Q2 Paper Review Outline (35 marks)

**Paper (even student number):** *LLM Collaboration with Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning*  
**Source PDF:** `a - LLM Collaboration with Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning.pdf`  
**Length:** ~1200–1500 words (2½–3 pages)  
**Guidelines:** Elsevier reviewer guide in `Material de apoio/Elsevier-A Brief Guide to Reviewing.pdf`

Do **not** cite the paper under review. Write in your own words. Provide evidence from the paper for each judgement.

## Suggested structure (matches marking scheme)

### 1. Structure, Language & Title (3)
- Is the title informative?
- Clarity of organisation and writing quality

### 2. Research Problem and Abstract (6)
- What problem do the authors address?
- Is the abstract complete and accurate?
- Motivation / significance

### 3. Literature Review (5)
- Coverage of related multi-agent RL / LLM collaboration work
- Gaps / missing baselines or citations

### 4. Methodology (10)
- Is the multi-agent + RL setup clear and justified?
- Reproducibility of algorithms, training protocol, environments
- Assumptions and design choices

### 5. Evaluation / Results (6)
- Are experiments adequate?
- Metrics, baselines, ablations
- Strength of claims vs evidence (no need to re-check statistics)

### 6. Conclusion / Discussion / Open Problems (5)
- Do conclusions match results?
- Limitations acknowledged?
- Useful open research questions?

## Tip from the brief
If something is missing or weak, say **how it could be improved**.
